SKODEX Chess – Privacy Policy

Last updated: 11 November 2026

SKODEX Chess ("the Game") is an Instant Game on Facebook and Messenger, published by SKODEX (Bojja Sanjay Kumar, India). This policy explains what information the Game uses and how you can have it deleted.

Information we use

  • Your Facebook player ID, display name and profile photo, provided by the Facebook Instant Games platform, to show your profile and leaderboard entries.
  • Game progress (wins, streak, rating, last played date), stored through Facebook's player data storage and your device's local storage.

We do not collect your email, phone number, location, contacts or payment details. We do not sell or share your information with third parties. Facebook's own processing is covered by the Meta Privacy Policy.

How we use it

Only to run the Game: save your progress, show your name and photo in the Game, and show leaderboards.

Data deletion

You can delete your Game data at any time:

  1. On Facebook, go to Settings & privacy → Settings → Apps and websites → Instant Games, select SKODEX Chess and choose Remove. This removes the Game's access and its stored player data.
  2. Or email skodexs@gmail.com with the subject "Delete my data". We will delete any data linked to your player ID within 30 days and confirm by email.

Children

The Game is not directed at children under 13 and we do not knowingly collect their information.

Changes

We may update this policy. The latest version is always on this page.
